## Description
After this story is complete, `agent-team-compliance.md` and the four ancillary skills (codex-review, codex-spec-review, multi-agent-patterns, agent-standards) use subagent vocabulary, with the removed `TeamCreate`/`TeamDelete` ceremony gone. One behaviorally-wrong line in `multi-agent-patterns/SKILL.md` (parallel-stories vs. serial staging) is corrected.

This is the heaviest of the three stories because `agent-team-compliance.md` is built around the explicit-team ceremony, yet its purpose is anti-fabrication (origin: E-076) — independent of the spawning mechanism. Pattern 1 must SURVIVE, reframed, not be removed. The filename is KEPT (user-confirmed). The ancillary skill edits are mostly mechanical vocabulary swaps. One line in `multi-agent-patterns/SKILL.md` (L55) is behaviorally wrong and is corrected here; the guardrail is to touch only that line, not the skill's broader (accurate) philosophy.
