# E-260-07: Add the context-layer ratchet tool + wire triggers 7/8 (mechanism 1)

## Epic

## Status
`DONE`

## Description
After this story is complete, the size of the context layer is measurable and bounded by a ratchet, and the two soft brakes that never held (trigger 7) and the promotion pump (trigger 8) are re-pointed at it. A new manual operator diagnostic, `.claude/hooks/context-ratchet.sh`, counts lines across the four subtrees (`.claude/rules`, `.claude/agents`, `.claude/skills`, `.claude/agent-memory`) and diffs against a committed baseline JSON, exiting non-zero on growth past baseline. Trigger 7 becomes the ratchet (not a soft prompt that accepts any answer); trigger 8's promote-to-load-target is gated on the ratchet (a promotion must cite the specific defect it demonstrably caught and fit the baseline); and `context-layer-guard.md`'s unenforced numeric targets fold into the baseline.

## Context
Epic Scope (Additions #1) and the trigger-7/trigger-8 analysis in Background: trigger 7 is already a soft counterweight that ran for the entire 1,652 → 4,061 growth and never produced a shrink, and trigger 8 is the promotion pump ("promote-to-load-target NOW") that fires every epic. Two brakes and a pump in one file; the pump wins because the brakes accept any answer. The ratchet is the mechanism trigger 7 declined to be — same instrument as E-257's reconciliation scoreboard, pointed at the configuration. Per operator decision 6, trigger 8 is GATED (not deleted): a promotion must cite the specific defect it caught and fit the baseline, and the Deletion-Side-Eviction + Memory-Retirement hygiene is kept. Per decision 8, the tool lives at `.claude/hooks/context-ratchet.sh` as a MANUAL operator diagnostic (not a registered hook), and the count glob is pinned to `*.md` + `*.sh` so the tool's count and AC-1's `git diff` agree.
