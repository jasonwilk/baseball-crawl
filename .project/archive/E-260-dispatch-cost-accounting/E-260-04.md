For the budget table, keep the table structure but refresh the counts and add the regenerating command (a line-count over the four subtrees). Because 04 and 07 are not ordered relative to each other, whichever lands second reconciles the reference — flag this in review; if 04 lands first, phrase the `:80` reference to the guard's durable placement-framework role rather than to a specific number.
