# E-238-02: Amend surface/delivery parity rules for quarantine

## Epic
[E-238: D1 — Quarantine + Navigation Retarget + Passkey Fix](../E-238-d1-quarantine-nav-passkey/epic.md)

## Status
`DONE`

## Description
After this story is complete, the four context-layer rule sites that encode "surface/delivery
parity" between the reports flow and the now-quarantined dashboard/scouting surfaces are
amended to reflect quarantine — reports is the sole forward surface, the parity constraints are
frozen/inert, and the protected-core shared seams are preserved. The aggregate-integrity parity
rule and references are demonstrably untouched.

## Context
Several rules treat the dashboard and the opponent/scouting pipeline as forward delivery
targets that new work must keep in parity with reports. Now that those surfaces are quarantined,
those parity constraints are no longer forward constraints. CA's discovery identified exactly
four surface-parity sites to amend and three aggregate-integrity NON-TARGETS that a naive grep
on "parity" would wrongly grab.
