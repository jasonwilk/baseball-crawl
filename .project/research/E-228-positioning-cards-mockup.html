E-228 Positioning Cards — Reference Mockup

This is a design reference for E-228-04. It demonstrates the landscape call sheet, the multi-up portrait player cards (2 × 3 grid), the design-only utility card variant, and the opponent-dashboard link card. Use your browser's Print → "Save as PDF" with mixed orientation supported (Chrome/Edge) to verify print fidelity.

1. Coach call sheet (landscape, 1 page)
Demonstrates all five AC-5 edge states across the rows: normal full-data, MIXED, partial-data (10-24 BIP, direction-only), thin (<10 BIP), and an all-TRUE batter group. Rows are sorted flagged-first (non-TRUE team_state_call), then by jersey number within each group. A 1px-gray separator between the two groups makes the boundary read as intentional.

3. Player cards (portrait, 2 × 3 grid, 6 cards = 1 sheet)
One card per covered fielding position (D3). Each card has the position name, the STRAIGHT UP default block, and an exceptions list keyed by opposing jersey number. The default block dominates each card visually so a glove-on player reading at arm’s length sees the most likely call instantly. MIXED batters carry a (MIXED) suffix on the call so fielders can match a coach’s “#23 MIXED” verbal call.
