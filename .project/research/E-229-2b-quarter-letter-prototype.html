E-229-2b — Quarter-letter Positioning Card Feasibility Prototype

Design reference for E-229-2b (no production code). Validates layout constants at exact 4.25" x 5.5" (quarter-letter, 4-up on letter portrait). Three card variants follow individually for type-and-spacing study, then a complete 2x2 sheet shows the print geometry end-to-end including the sheet-2 fill slots (compass key + opponent context card).

Companion spec is the canonical source of truth: .project/research/E-229-locked-layout-constants.md.

1. Full-data card (50+ BIPs, 5 outliers, density bg, BIP-count caption)
Eastlake Bears, LF position. Team has 142 BIPs (full tier). Star is solid filled with "(142 BIP)" caption. Textbook reference dot offset from star. Density-bg dots scattered across the field at ~12% opacity. Compass letters at fixed angular positions; populated zones at full opacity, empty zones at 30% opacity placeholders. Five outlier pills positioned at their per-batter centroids.
