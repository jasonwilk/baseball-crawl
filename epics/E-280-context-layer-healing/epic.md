## Overview
The fleet's wall-clock cost per shipped story roughly tripled between early July and late July 2026, and two independent transcript analyses agree the cause is the process layer, not the model. This epic implements the four operator-ruled trims, two structural cleanups, and — added 2026-08-02 — the retirement of the context-layer size gate in favour of a scheduled periodic refinement pass, under one binding design principle: **every change must SHRINK the error-generating surface** — fewer restatements, structure in place of prose, verification at authoring time — rather than merely cutting review cost. Restatement is this repo's dominant defect generator, so each fact this epic touches ends up stated once with pointers to it.

## Background & Context

### What was measured
Two independent analyses of the same transcript corpus, with different scripts and different metric definitions, compared only after both were complete. **Both source files are ON DISK, UNTRACKED, and EPHEMERAL by operator ruling — they will be deleted.** Every number this epic relies on is therefore quoted inline in Technical Notes TN-1 and TN-2 rather than cited by path. Their verdict ordering: **process ≫ pathology > model ≈ 0**.

The E-279 dispatch was then decomposed directly (TN-3) and showed the same shape at story grain: 66% of active time was review and verification, and one story spent 14 minutes on implementation against a 94-minute verification tail.

### Why this is a context-layer epic, not a policy memo
The cost lives in files. `.claude/rules/` grew from 165KB to 307KB between 2026-06-27 and 2026-07-29 (TN-2), and median end-of-session main-agent context grew 391k → 554k tokens across the same window. The trims are edits to specific rules, skills, and agent definitions; the design owner (claude-architect) locates the seams and picks the mechanisms, and PM frames the acceptance criteria and owns scope and sequencing.

### What this epic is NOT reacting to
The model. Reverting the fleet to Opus 4.8 has a measured upside of **at most 1.4x on dispatch cost, and that figure is a ceiling confounded with epic mix** (TN-1). Corrected against a constant-model bridge control, Opus 5 is at parity or slightly faster (TN-2). Opus 5 stays.

## Goals
- Each of the four trims is expressed as a **property of the workflow files** that a reader can check against the files alone, without knowing who edited them or in what order.
- The delivery-confirmation economy is single-sourced **one-of-each**: exactly one completion report, one review verdict from code-reviewer, and one AC verdict from PM per story, each issued once against a frozen story state and not re-askable. PM's AC authority is preserved (OQ-5, operator-ruled).
- Per-story review effort is tiered by **file-path class** — a mechanical test — never by an agent's judgment call about risk.
- The heaviest always-loaded rule file is materially smaller in bytes with **every rule it states still present**, verified by a per-rule inventory rather than by an impression of the diff.
- All ten `SKILL.md` files carry `name` and `description` frontmatter, and CLAUDE.md's Workflows section is compressed without losing a trigger phrase.
- The context-layer size **gate** is retired and replaced by a scheduled periodic refinement pass; the measurement survives as an on-demand diagnostic, and no file in the layer still describes a gate, a baseline, or an exception ritual that no longer exists.

### TN-5: Distillation — what "every RULE survives" has to mean
"Compress carefully" is an action, not a property, and is satisfiable by compressing badly. The checkable form is a **per-rule inventory**: enumerate the rules stated in `.claude/rules/tool-output-integrity.md` before the change, and after the change record a per-rule verdict naming where each one now lives. A rule with no surviving location is a failure; a rule that moved to a pointer is a pass with its target named. The inventory is the artifact the verdict is read from, and its row count against the pre-change enumeration is what makes an omission visible. Incident narratives compress to pointer or evidence form — and per `.claude/rules/tool-output-integrity.md`'s own criterion-vs-evidence cut, a figure a reader must *see what was observed* is evidence and is preserved verbatim, while a figure a reader must *meet* is a criterion and may be restated.

### TN-7: Design principle (operator-ratified) and its test
Every change must shrink the error-generating surface, not merely cut review cost. The test to apply per change: *after this edit, is there one fewer place where a fact is restated?* A change that adds a new rule to enforce an old one fails this test even if it saves review time.
