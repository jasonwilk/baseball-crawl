## Description
After this story, no file in the context layer asserts that the layer's size is gated, bounded by a committed baseline, or subject to an operator-signed-exception ritual. In its place the layer states two cadences in one file: a **scheduled periodic context-layer refinement pass** (replacing continuous gating) and the **batched audit cadence** (TRIM 2). `.claude/hooks/context-ratchet.sh` survives untouched as an on-demand diagnostic.

## Context

The measurement is kept and only the one-way gate goes — the same disposition the operator gave the reconcile-scoreboard ratchet on 2026-07-26, where the scoreboard survived as a diagnostic and the gate against a committed baseline was retired.

**What made the gate indefensible was structural, not anybody's conduct.** `context-ratchet.sh` is an unregistered manual diagnostic that accrues continuously and is charged to whichever epic closes next. E-279 accrued +286 lines in 22h33m of elapsed planning time and contributed +64 at dispatch, failing at +350; the live tree measured +418 on 2026-08-01, of which **89% was `agent-memory`** — a subtree that grows as a function of doing work, against a fixed total. An epic closing more than about a day after a snapshot fails by construction, whatever its own discipline. Epic TN-9 carries that reading in full; preserve it there as evidence and do not update it.

**TRIM 2 arrived here from E-280-04 on 2026-08-02**, because the gate seams and the audit-cadence line land in the same file and a single story is the file-disjoint home for both. Its quality risk is the highest of the four trims in the primary analysis's own words — *"these audits found a CRITICAL; reduce frequency, don't delete"* — which is why AC-4 exists.

## Technical Approach
Enumerate the sites by search **before** editing anything, and report the enumeration — AC-6 is checkable only against a pre-registered list, and reconstructing one from the diff afterwards is the failure mode this repo's 8-of-8 author-detection record predicts.

Epic TN-15 carries PM's own search results as a floor and, as importantly, the sites PM checked and ruled OUT with the reason. Read it before searching so the **five** `implement/SKILL.md` false positives do not consume a round. (⚠️ This sentence said "four" until 2026-08-02 — **the count-propagation defect this epic exists to fix, living inside the story that fixes it, and the third instance of it in this planning round.** The Context section and AC-13 were corrected and this line was not. Codex caught it.)

`.claude/rules/doc-sweep.md` governs the sweep, and its retired-claim section is directly on point rather than generically applicable: enumerate the **judgements that depended on the gate** — the ratings, the "there is no separate target" concessions, the cadence-versus-size framing — not merely the rephrasings of it.

The two cadences are different things and should read as different things: the batched audit cadence governs how often audits run; the periodic refinement pass governs how often the layer is deliberately pruned. Placing them in one file is a single-sourcing decision, not a claim that they are one mechanism.
