## Context
The routing-era hypothesis (§3.3) predicts that after E-251 replaced bare `general-purpose` spawns with defined agents, reviewer-quality defect classes narrow while structural classes do not. The empirical check is a re-tally of the CR-vs-Codex finding gap across ~5 epics that close under the canonical Review Scorecard schema (which landed in E-255). As of 2026-07-08 that precondition is definitively NOT met: only four epics (E-252/253/254/255) have closed since E-251, and the canonical scorecard schema landed only in E-255 today — so zero epics have yet closed under it. A per-closure recorded-verdict gate would be noise (it no-ops for 5 epics, fires once, then is stale forever); the right mechanism is a PM-owned tracked item with a countable fire-condition, surfaced by PM's existing every-closure memory/idea review. The trigger's home, fire-condition, and procedure are specified in epic Technical Notes TN-6.
